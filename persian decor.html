Examples of Persian Decor in Every Room

Save for Later!

Persian decor is renowned for its rich history, intricate patterns, and vibrant colors. Infusing your home with Persian-inspired design can create a warm, elegant, and culturally rich atmosphere. Here are 15 tips to help you achieve an exquisite Persian decor for each room of your house.

1. Persian Living Room: Luxurious Rugs and Pillows

A Persian living room starts with a foundation of luxurious Persian rugs, known for their intricate designs and high-quality craftsmanship. These rugs serve as the centerpiece of the room, adding warmth, texture, and a splash of color. Choose rugs with rich hues such as deep reds, blues, and golds to anchor your living space.

Enhance the comfort and style of your living room with an array of decorative pillows in complementary colors and patterns. Look for pillows featuring traditional Persian motifs such as paisleys, florals, and geometric designs. Arrange them generously on sofas and armchairs to create a cozy and inviting seating area.

Complete the look with elegant coffee tables and side tables made from dark woods or brass. Incorporate elements like brass lamps, ornate mirrors, and Persian-inspired artwork to create a space that is both luxurious and culturally rich.

2. Persian Dining Room: Ornate Furniture and Tableware

In a Persian dining room, the table and chairs are often the focal points. Choose a dining set with intricate woodwork, preferably in dark woods such as walnut or mahogany. Carved details and inlays with mother of pearl or brass will add an extra layer of opulence and authenticity.

Set the table with traditional Persian tableware, including hand-painted plates, delicate tea glasses, and ornate silverware. Use table runners and placemats with Persian patterns to add depth and texture to the dining experience. Don’t forget a striking centerpiece like a brass candelabra or a vase with fresh or dried flowers.

Ambient lighting plays a crucial role in creating a warm and inviting dining atmosphere. Opt for chandeliers or pendant lights with intricate designs, and consider adding wall sconces for a soft, diffused glow. Persian rugs under the dining table can also enhance the room’s visual appeal and comfort.

3. Persian Kitchen: Tiles and Textiles

Bring Persian charm into your kitchen with vibrant and intricate tilework. Use Persian tiles for backsplashes, countertops, or even flooring to infuse the space with color and pattern. The traditional designs often feature geometric shapes, florals, and calligraphy, creating a stunning visual impact.

Incorporate textiles such as Persian-style table runners, curtains, and dish towels. These elements can add warmth and texture to your kitchen, making it feel more homely and inviting. Choose fabrics with rich colors and traditional Persian patterns to maintain a cohesive look.

Add decorative items like brass pots, handcrafted ceramic bowls, and colorful spice jars to complete the look. Display them on open shelves or countertops for easy access and aesthetic appeal. These details not only enhance the Persian vibe but also add functionality to your kitchen.

4. Persian Bedroom: Opulent Textiles and Patterns

Create a serene and luxurious Persian-inspired bedroom with opulent textiles and intricate patterns. Start with a statement bedspread or duvet cover featuring traditional Persian designs in rich colors such as deep reds, blues, and golds. Complement this with an array of decorative pillows and throws to add layers of texture and comfort.

Choose a Persian rug that extends beyond the bed, creating a plush and inviting surface underfoot. The intricate patterns and vibrant colors will tie the room together and provide a sense of luxury. Curtains with similar patterns or complementary colors can further enhance the aesthetic.

Add traditional Persian furniture pieces such as a carved wooden bed frame, nightstands, and dressers. Incorporate decorative items like brass lamps, ornate mirrors, and Persian artwork to complete the look. The result will be a bedroom that feels both elegant and culturally rich.

5. Persian Bathroom: Elegant Fixtures and Details

Transform your bathroom into a Persian-inspired oasis with elegant fixtures and intricate details. Choose a vanity with ornate woodwork or inlaid with mother of pearl or brass. A mirror with a decorative frame can add an extra layer of sophistication and cultural charm.

Incorporate Persian tiles in the shower area, backsplash, or flooring. The intricate patterns and vibrant colors will add a unique touch to your bathroom. Use brass fixtures and fittings to enhance the overall aesthetic and provide a touch of luxury.

Accessorize with Persian-inspired elements such as patterned towels, decorative soap dispensers, and brass or ceramic containers. Add a small Persian rug or runner to complete the look and provide a soft, warm surface underfoot.

6. Persian Home Office: Rich Textures and Colors

Create a productive and inspiring home office with Persian decor elements. Start with a sturdy wooden desk with intricate carvings or inlaid details. Pair it with a comfortable chair upholstered in rich fabrics or leather.

Enhance the space with a Persian rug under the desk, adding a touch of luxury and comfort. Use rich colors such as deep reds, blues, and golds to create a warm and inviting atmosphere. Incorporate storage solutions like bookshelves or cabinets with traditional Persian designs.

Accessorize with Persian-inspired elements such as brass desk lamps, ornate picture frames, and decorative containers. Add artwork featuring Persian motifs or calligraphy to complete the look. The result will be a home office that feels both elegant and culturally rich.

7. Persian Entryway: Welcoming and Elegant

Create a welcoming and elegant entryway with Persian decor elements. Start with a beautiful Persian rug to set the tone for the rest of the home. Choose a rug with intricate patterns and rich colors to make a strong first impression.

Add a console table with carved details or inlaid with mother of pearl or brass. Use the table to display decorative items such as brass bowls, vases with fresh or dried flowers, and Persian-inspired artwork. A mirror with a decorative frame can also add an extra layer of sophistication.

Enhance the space with ambient lighting from wall sconces or table lamps. Use Persian-style textiles for cushions on a bench or chair to add comfort and style. The result will be an entryway that feels both welcoming and culturally rich.

8. Persian Patio: Outdoor Elegance

Bring Persian elegance to your outdoor space with carefully chosen decor elements. Start with a Persian-style rug to define the seating area and add a touch of luxury. Choose outdoor furniture with intricate details and comfortable cushions in rich fabrics.

Add decorative elements such as brass lanterns, ceramic pots, and potted plants to create a lush and inviting atmosphere. Use textiles such as throw pillows and blankets with traditional Persian patterns to enhance the comfort and style of the space.

Incorporate a water feature such as a fountain or a small pond to add a sense of tranquility. Use ambient lighting from string lights or lanterns to create a warm and inviting ambiance. The result will be a patio that feels both elegant and culturally rich.

9. Persian Children’s Room: Playful Patterns and Colors

Create a playful and culturally rich environment for your children with Persian decor elements. Start with a vibrant Persian rug to anchor the room and provide a soft, comfortable surface for play. Choose rugs with bright colors and playful patterns to create a cheerful atmosphere.

Incorporate furniture with traditional Persian designs, such as carved wooden beds, dressers, and bookshelves. Use colorful textiles for bedding, curtains, and pillows, featuring traditional Persian motifs and patterns. These elements will create a fun and visually stimulating environment for your children.

Add decorative items such as wall art, mobiles, and toys with Persian themes. Use storage solutions like woven baskets or painted wooden boxes to keep the room organized while adding a touch of cultural charm. The result will be a children’s room that is both playful and rich in cultural heritage.

10. Persian Guest Room: Hospitable and Stylish

Create a hospitable and stylish guest room with Persian decor elements. Start with a comfortable bed featuring a headboard with intricate carvings or inlaid details. Use luxurious bedding with traditional Persian patterns and rich colors to create a welcoming atmosphere.

Add a Persian rug to anchor the room and provide a warm, inviting surface. Use matching curtains to enhance the room’s overall aesthetic. Consider adding a comfortable armchair or chaise longue with complementary textiles for a cozy seating area.

Incorporate decorative elements such as brass lamps, ornate mirrors, and Persian artwork. Provide your guests with essentials like a tray with tea set, fresh flowers, and toiletries in decorative containers. The result will be a guest room that feels both elegant and welcoming.

11. Persian Library: Cultural Richness and Comfort

Create a library or reading nook that exudes cultural richness and comfort with Persian decor elements. Start with built-in bookshelves or freestanding units made from dark woods with intricate carvings. Arrange your book collection alongside decorative items such as brass bookends, vases, and framed artwork.

Add a Persian rug to define the reading area and provide a cozy, comfortable surface. Choose a plush armchair or chaise longue upholstered in rich fabrics for a luxurious seating option. Enhance the comfort with decorative pillows and throws in complementary colors and patterns.

Incorporate ambient lighting from floor lamps or table lamps with ornate designs. Use Persian-style textiles for curtains or window treatments to add warmth and style. The result will be a library that feels both sophisticated and culturally rich.

12. Persian Home Theater: Luxurious and Inviting

Transform your home theater into a luxurious and inviting space with Persian decor elements. Start with comfortable seating options such as plush sofas or recliners upholstered in rich fabrics. Arrange them around a large screen for optimal viewing.

Add a Persian rug to anchor the seating area and provide a warm, comfortable surface. Use rich colors and intricate patterns to enhance the room’s overall aesthetic. Incorporate decorative pillows and throws to add layers of comfort and style.

Enhance the ambiance with ambient lighting from wall sconces or recessed lighting. Use Persian-style textiles for curtains or window treatments to block out light and create a cozy atmosphere. The result will be a home theater that feels both luxurious and inviting.

13. Persian Laundry Room: Functional and Stylish

Elevate your laundry room with Persian decor elements to make it both functional and stylish. Start with practical storage solutions such as cabinets or shelves with intricate details. Use baskets and bins made from natural materials to keep the space organized.

Add a small Persian rug or runner to provide a warm, comfortable surface underfoot. Choose vibrant colors and patterns to brighten the space and add visual interest. Use Persian-style textiles for curtains or window treatments to enhance the room’s overall aesthetic.

Incorporate decorative elements such as brass hooks, ceramic containers, and framed artwork. These touches will add cultural charm and make the space more enjoyable to use. The result will be a laundry room that feels both functional and stylish.

14. Persian Garden: Lush and Tranquil

Create a lush and tranquil garden with Persian decor elements. Start with a layout that incorporates traditional Persian garden design principles, such as symmetry and the use of water features. Add pathways made from natural stone or tiles with intricate patterns.

Incorporate a variety of plants, including flowering shrubs, trees, and potted plants. Use ceramic or terracotta pots with Persian-inspired designs to add visual interest. Create seating areas with comfortable furniture and Persian-style textiles for cushions and throws.

Add decorative elements such as brass lanterns, ceramic tiles, and fountains. Use ambient lighting from string lights or lanterns to create a warm, inviting atmosphere. The result will be a garden that feels both lush and tranquil, perfect for relaxation and enjoyment.

15. Persian Staircase: Grand and Inviting

Transform your staircase into a grand and inviting feature with Persian decor elements. Start with a runner featuring intricate patterns and rich colors to add visual interest and comfort underfoot. Choose a design that complements the overall aesthetic of your home.

Add decorative elements such as brass or wrought iron railing details to enhance the staircase’s elegance. Use framed Persian artwork or mirrors along the walls to create a sense of grandeur and cultural richness.

Incorporate ambient lighting from wall sconces or pendant lights to highlight the staircase’s design. Use Persian-style textiles for any nearby windows or seating areas to create a cohesive look. The result will be a staircase that feels both grand and inviting.
